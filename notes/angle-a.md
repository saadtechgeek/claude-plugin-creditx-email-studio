# Angle A: calm and in control of your money

Subject line options for email 01 (CreditX Advisor launch). Each is 50 characters or fewer.

1. Choose your card calmly, on your own terms (42 characters)
2. A calmer way to pick your next credit card (42 characters)
3. You stay in control of your next card (37 characters)

## Brand check

- No banned words: no "guaranteed", "pre-approved", "act now" or "last chance".
- No urgency, no fear, no promised approval, and no numbers.
- No mention of anyone's score.
- All three stay on the "calm and in control" idea. None promises an outcome.
