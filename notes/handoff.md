# Handoff: email 01 subject angles
- Credit builders: a card that fits where they are, with no judgment. They worry about being turned down.
- Rewards seekers: a simple match to how they spend. They worry about picking wrong or too many options.
- Premium spenders: a quick, fair comparison of travel or premium cards, without wasted effort.
- Angle A, "calm and in control": 3 subject lines, e.g. "A calmer way to pick your next credit card".
- Angle B, "options that fit you": 3 subject lines, e.g. "See which credit card options may fit you".
- Both pass the brand check (no banned words, no numbers, no score mentions, all 50 characters or fewer).
- Pick: Angle B. It suits all three groups and uses our voice words ("options", "fit", "may").
- Angle A's "calm" fits credit builders best, but it is narrower: B speaks to every group.
- Suggested subject: "See which credit card options may fit you" (41 characters). Test A as the variant.
