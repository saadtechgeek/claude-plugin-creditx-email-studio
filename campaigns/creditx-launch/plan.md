# CreditX Advisor Launch: Campaign Plan

Status: draft for approval. Not sent. All practice sends go to @example.com addresses only.

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Name:** "Your Card, Matched" (working name)
- **Summary:** Over four weeks, email our waitlist so people try CreditX Advisor and get a card suggestion that fits their situation.
- **Primary goal:** 300 waitlist members start using CreditX Advisor within the first month (Mon 2026-12-07 to Sun 2027-01-03). That is 15% of about 2,000 people.
- **"Start using" means:** the person finishes the first step in the advisor and sees at least one card suggestion. The team should confirm this definition before launch.
- **Secondary goals:**
  - Keep unsubscribes low and trust high.
  - Learn which group responds best, so later campaigns can lean on it.

## 2. Audience

About 2,000 waitlist members, in three groups. Emails speak to each group's situation. They never state or hint at a person's own score.

| Group | Who they are | What they worry about | What they want | Buying stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Credit builders (scores under 670) | Working adults building credit | Being turned down, feeling judged | A card that fits where they are, with no shame | Consideration |
| Rewards seekers (670-769) | Solid credit, everyday spenders | Picking the wrong rewards card, too many options | A simple match for how they spend | Consideration |
| Premium spenders (770 and up) | Strong credit, higher spending | Wasting effort comparing premium options | A quick, fair comparison for travel or big spending | Consideration to decision |

All three are on the waitlist already, so they know the brand. The job is to move them from "interested" to "tried it."

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** Pick the right card without comparing dozens. CreditX Advisor suggests cards that fit you.

**Supporting messages**

1. **It is quick.** One idea, a few minutes, no spreadsheets. (Proof: the first step is short; confirm the real time before we write it.)
2. **It fits you.** Suggestions follow your situation and spending, not a one-size-fits-all list. (Proof: the advisor matches your biggest spending area to a card's benefit.)
3. **It is fair and calm.** We suggest, the issuer decides, and we say so every time. (Proof: the line "CreditX Advisor suggests cards. The card issuer makes the approval decision." appears in every email.)
4. **No pressure.** Try it when you are ready. (Proof: no countdowns, no urgency language.)

**Tone by group**

- Builders: warm and steady. Use "building credit". Never use shaming words.
- Rewards seekers: practical and simple. Focus on matching a card to everyday spending.
- Premium spenders: brief and respectful. Focus on a fast, fair comparison.

## 4. Channel strategy

| Channel | Why | Format | Effort | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Email (practice server only) | Our waitlist is already on email, and the budget allows nothing else | Short plain-text-style emails, one button each | Low to medium | Staff time only |
| Launch page (owned) | Every email needs one place to land | Single page with one "Start" button | Medium, one-time | Staff time only |

No paid ads, no social, no PR. Only email is in scope.

## 7. Success metrics

**Primary:** 300 waitlist members start using CreditX Advisor by Sun 2027-01-03 (15% of about 2,000).

**Secondary**

| Metric | How tracked | Starting target (adjust after week 1) |
|---|---|---|
| Starts per group | Count from the advisor, split by group | Roughly in line with group size |
| Click-through on the "Start" button | Email tool link clicks | Track and compare across emails |
| Open rate | Email tool | Track only; not a goal on its own |
| Unsubscribe rate | Email tool | Stay low; pause and review if it climbs after any email |
| Replies or questions | Inbox | Use them to improve emails 5 to 10 |

Targets are starting guesses, not proven benchmarks. We have no past campaign data to compare against.

**Reporting:** quick check every Monday (starts so far vs. 300); final report Sun 2027-01-03.

**Pace check:** to reach 300 by the end, we need about 75 starts a week. If fewer than 100 people have started by Mon 2026-12-21, review the plan.

## 9. Risks and what to do

| Risk | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Fewer than 300 start.** The 15% goal may be high for a first email run. | Check pace each Monday. Use the open slot and the group examples to push on the group that is behind. |
| **Holiday weeks lower attention** (Dec 21 to Jan 3). | Keep emails short, avoid Dec 24, Dec 25 and Dec 31, and stop sending to people who already started. |
| **A claim slips past the brand rules** (approval, numbers, score, repair). | Run every email through brand-review. Check for "guaranteed", "pre-approved", "act now" and "last chance". Never read a person's score back. |
| **Wrong people get an email** (already started, or a real person). | Use the "has started" flag. Practice sends go to @example.com only. |
