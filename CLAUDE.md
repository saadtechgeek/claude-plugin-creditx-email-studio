# Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business. The first client is CreditX Advisor, a made-up AI credit-card advisor launching to its waitlist. Claude plans each campaign, writes the emails, personalizes them for each customer group, and sends them only to a practice email server.

## Folders

- `brand/` - the brand voice guide: how the brand sounds, words to use and avoid, and claims we must never make.
- `leads/` - practice customers (made-up people only) with their group and consent.
- `campaigns/` - one folder per campaign, holding its plan and its emails.
- `notes/` - working notes and research, such as which skills I chose and why.

## Rules

- Write in plain English. No jargon.
- Never send email to real people. This studio uses made-up people at @example.com only.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
