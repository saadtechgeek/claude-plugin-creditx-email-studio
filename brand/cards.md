# CreditX Advisor: card catalog (made-up)

All cards, fees, rates and limits below are **fictional practice data**. They are not real products and not real offers. Cards 1-9 are the catalog from the CreditX Advisor app (project 07). Cards 10-15 are added so every credit score band has something to recommend.

CreditX Advisor is an advisor, not a bank. It suggests cards that fit a person's score and spending. It does not approve or decline applications. The card issuer makes that decision.

## Credit score bands

| Band | Score | Plain-English meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Rebuilding | 300-579 | History has serious gaps or missed payments. Best next step is a card built for rebuilding. |
| Fair | 580-669 | Some gaps. Starter and student-style cards fit. |
| Good | 670-739 | Solid history. Most everyday rewards cards are in reach. |
| Very good | 740-799 | Strong history. Category cards and premium travel cards fit. |
| Exceptional | 800-850 | Excellent history. The best-rewards cards fit. |

A band is a guide, not a promise. Past missed payments, income and debt also matter.

## Who should get what: quick guide by score

| Score band | Cards to suggest, in order |
|---|---|
| Rebuilding (300-579) | Fresh Start Secured, Second Chance Builder |
| Fair (580-669) | Stepping Stone Starter, Student Builder (if a student), Fresh Start Secured as a safer start |
| Good (670-739) | FlatCash Everyday for simplicity, or the category card that matches their top spending (HomeBills Saver, FreshCart Rewards, FuelSaver Gold, ShowTime Entertainer). Cash Cushion if they carry a balance. |
| Very good (740-799) | The category card that matches their biggest spending (ShopSphere Platinum, DineDelight Signature), or a travel card (Globetrotter Plus, Voyager Elite) if travel is their biggest spend. If rent is the biggest spend, HomeBills Saver. |
| Exceptional (800+) | Summit Reserve, or Voyager Elite for heavy travelers |

## Matching by spending

The advisor looks at nine spending categories (travel, fuel, shopping, groceries, restaurants, medicine, rent, utilities, entertainment) and picks the card whose benefit matches the customer's largest category. Ties go to the card with the lower annual fee. A card is never suggested when its minimum score is above the customer's score.

## The advisor's own rules (from the app)

The original app's eligibility engine only suggests a card when the score is at least 750 and there is at most 1 past default. That is deliberately cautious. This catalog extends the app downward: cards 10-13 (and the Good-band cards) give lower scores something to be suggested, using the guide above. For scores of 750 and up, the original rule still applies.

## What we must never say in marketing

- Never promise approval ("guaranteed", "pre-approved", "you'll be accepted").
- Never quote a specific rate, limit or fee as what a person will get. Rates and limits depend on the issuer and the person.
- Never say a score will go up by a set amount or in a set time.
- Never say a card "fixes", "repairs" or "erases" bad credit.
- Always say CreditX Advisor suggests cards, and the issuer decides.
